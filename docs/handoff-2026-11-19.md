## Four sequences to prepare

1. Normal finish: win and loss; normal completion plus interrupted-return display and explicit Spin permission.
2. Lost response after debit: fresh login/game-info finds original round; same-token retry; amount/mode conflict.
3. Interrupted feature: released free spin with counter/keys/cumulative win; chest safe boundary; repeated/two-tab confirmation and no double progression.
4. 30-day expiry: polls do not renew; server close; uncertain payment hides amount and blocks Spin; confirmed payout -> final summary -> dismiss + duplicate retry -> next bet allowed.

Unresolved field names such as `wallet.confirmedAt` are suggestions, not client-approved names. Do not hard-code fake payment success to bypass the uncertainty state. Keep identifiers, multipliers, counters and currency amounts distinct. Final Kai math remains pending. A owns the server/wallet contract.

## Existing presentation policies to preserve

Landing loads game-info before Continue. Pending `intro`: Continue enters normal, automatically replays recorded Scatter win then bonus intro, with no Resume popup. Later feature checkpoint: Continue enters feature scene, then shows Resume before continuing. Normal-round interrupted flow is the newly identified missing path; do not conflate it with feature intro.

Big Win through Max Win uses full viewport canvas, an upward-configured center and a wrapped amount object so Spine does not overwrite responsive amount scaling. Long KRW values have desktop/portrait/landscape tests. `checkWinDelayMs`, `allowForceStop=false` for Tavern, Drop default, masks and shared landing motion were already implemented.
